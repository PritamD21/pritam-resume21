M.Sc project · VNIT Nagpur · 2016–2017

Quantum tunnelling and transmission

I studied transmission coefficients and tunnelling phase time for multiple symmetrical potential barriers using the transfer matrix method. The work involved numerical computing in Python and C++ and the phenomenon of revival of transparency.

Summer research · TIFR Mumbai · 2016

Particle-physics data analysis

As a summer research student in the Department of High Energy Particle Physics, I worked on an India-based Neutrino Observatory project using a 12-layer resistive plate chamber detector. I studied sea-level muon flux and detector efficiency using ROOT and C++.
